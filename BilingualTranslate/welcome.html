Welcome to Vimi Bilingual

Read the web in two languages and turn what you read into vocabulary you actually remember — 100% on your device.

Heads up: your browser may not support on-device translation

Vimi uses Chrome's built-in Translator, which needs Chrome or Edge 138+. Saving words, reviews, tests and the dashboard work either way; full-page translation needs the supported browser.

How to start in 30 seconds

  1. 1 Translate a page — open any article and press Alt+T, or click the Vimi toolbar icon. The translation appears under each paragraph. (First time, the model downloads once.)
  2. 2 Save a word — highlight any word or phrase on a page and click Save in the little popup (or right-click → “Add to Vimi vocab”). The sentence it came from is saved as context.
  3. 3 Review & test — Vimi schedules each word with spaced repetition and sets up periodic tests, so words stick.

Make browsing teach you

In Settings → General → Reading aids you can highlight words you've saved when you meet them again, sprinkle a few translations into pages, or hide translations until you hover. And in Backup & restore, keep a copy of everything safe.

Translating Local Documents?

Cannot translate local files directly in Chrome? No worries! Use our free web companion Doc2Notion (https://doc2-notion.vercel.app/) to read & translate your PDFs, Word, Excel, and Markdown documents bilingually with Vimi.

Everything — translation, saved words, reviews — stays on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.
